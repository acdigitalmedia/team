Critique · GB Fixit homepage (Blueprint)

Reviewed: .open-design/gb-fixit-handyman-services-homepage-gbfixit-co-u/merged.html · Reviewer: OD · Critique skill · 29 Oct 2026

Verdict: clean, on-brand and functional, but the blueprint theme is a costume worn over a stock marketing template. It reads "competent AI landing page" because every section is built the same way and nothing on the page comes from Kris and Gary's actual world.

1 · Philosophy consistency

5

The blueprint idea is decoration, not structure. "Sheet 02–08" kickers, "Fig. 01 · Kris" callouts and GBF-01 part numbers are labels stuck onto a standard marketing layout; the callouts point at an empty frame, not at anything measured. The services section then mixes in Workshop-dark's giant 01–06 numerals, so each tile is numbered twice (01 and GBF-01). Yellow, meant for calls to action only, appears in every kicker, rule, icon and number, so it stops meaning "act".

FixEither make the blueprint real (dimensions, plans, before/after measured) or drop the props; pick one numbering system.

2 · Visual hierarchy

5

The hero is strong (112px "HANDYMAN IN DUNDEE"). Below it, all seven sections use one template: yellow kicker, 64px uppercase H2, grey paragraph on the right, then a box grid. With equal weight everywhere the eye gets no rest and no climax; recent work (the most persuasive content for a tradesman) gets the same treatment as "how we work". In the hero, the dim line, callouts, specbar and two CTAs all compete under the headline.

FixGive one section below the hero a different scale entirely (full-bleed photo band or one giant pull-quote).

3 · Detail execution

7

Grids align, borders are consistent at 1px, type tracking is uniform and the phone layout now fits at 390px. Misses: gallery frames get only two corner marks (.c1, .c4) while every other frame gets four; the six review tiles end at different heights inside the joined grid; the top bar's long claim wraps to two lines on phones; header and footer both repeat the logo on a navy box that sits on a navy background.

KeepThe joined 1px-gap tile grids and the consistent uppercase label system.

4 · Functionality

7

Free-quote CTA and tap-to-call are above the fold; sticky header; phone call bar; every service and area links to its new page; dropdowns open on focus as well as hover. Weak spots: the form is a mock (Wix form will replace it); 13 photo placeholders mean the page cannot yet prove the work; the 112px H1 pushes the intro below the fold on 13-inch laptops.

KeepCTA placement, tel links, and the service and area link structure (it's what the SEO plan needs).

5 · Innovation

3

This is where "generic Claude website" comes from. A faint grid background, a 3×2 card grid, a 4-step row, a 3×2 testimonial grid, eyebrow + big heading + side paragraph, logo left and nav right: every element is the median landing page. Nothing on the page could only belong to GB Fixit: no van, no tools, no before/after, no Dundee street, no handwriting, no phone number big enough to read across a room. The one distinctive idea (blueprint) is common in AI output and is used as wallpaper.

FixBuild one signature moment from the business itself: a full-width before/after strip, the van as the hero, or the phone number set as the biggest type on the page.

Keep

  • Navy + #FFDE11 palette and uppercase Barlow Semi Condensed headings.
  • H1 "Handyman in Dundee" and the SEO headings carried from the live site.
  • Service tiles where the number lights up on hover.
  • Trust figures (9.8/10, 5.0, Trusted Trader) high on the page.
  • Phone call bar and tap-to-call everywhere.

Fix

  • Break the repeated section template: vary scale, width and alignment.
  • Restrict yellow to the main CTA plus one signature element.
  • Remove the decorative blueprint props (Sheet/Fig/GBF) or make them mean something.
  • Make real job photos structural (before/after band), not small boxes.
  • One numbering system for services.

Quick wins

  • Turn the six review tiles into one oversized pull-quote plus a short list.
  • Set 07740 443180 as a huge type moment above the footer.
  • Drop the faint grid on lower sections so the hero owns it.
  • Shorten the top-bar claim so it fits one line on phones.
