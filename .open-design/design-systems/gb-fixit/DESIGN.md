# Design System Inspired by GB Fixit

> Category: Local Trades & Home Services
> Navy and safety-yellow trade branding: clean white and warm-cream sections, Jost headings, Barlow text, plain and trustworthy.

## Color Palette
- **Navy #0E4D82**: primary brand colour. Header bar, headings, outline buttons, icons, table heads.
- **Fixit Yellow #FECC00**: calls to action only (Get a quote, Call Kris). Always navy text on yellow, never white.
- **Deep Navy #0B1D30 / #0F2236**: footer and dark bands; link hover colour.
- **Mid Blue #2F6BA3**: the reviews band and secondary dark surfaces.
- **Cream #F5F2EA**: alternate section background; form panels. Deeper cream #E9E5DA / #D9D3C4 for photo placeholders and chips.
- **White #FFFFFF**: main page background and cards.
- **Ink #1F2933**: body text. **Slate #4A5562**: captions, meta, breadcrumbs.
- **Border #E3DED2**: card edges and row dividers; input borders #B9B2A3.

## Typography
- **Display: Jost** (Google Fonts), weight 500 (600 for small headings). H1 52px / 1.05, H2 34px, H3 22–24px. No letter-spacing on headings.
- **Body: Barlow** (Google Fonts), 400 body at 17px / 1.6, 500–700 for UI.
- **Labels and buttons**: Barlow 600, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing 1px; eyebrow labels 14px with 1.5px tracking in Slate.
- Big numbers in trust strips (9.8/10, 5.0 ★) set in Jost 500, 26–40px, navy.

## Voice & Tone
Plain, friendly Dundee tradesman. Speak as "we" (Kris and Gary), name real places (Dundee, Broughty Ferry, Monifieth, Carnoustie) and real jobs. Promise concrete things: a written quote, start and finish when we say, tidy at the end of every day. No hype words, no invented stats; ratings come from Reference Line and Google only.

## Imagery & Iconography
Real job photos: before/afters, finished rooms, fences, gardens, Kris and Gary at work. No stock photos of models. Where a photo is missing, use a labelled cream placeholder like [PHOTO: new fence and gate]. Icons are simple 2px-stroke line icons in navy (phone, check, arrow); stars in yellow on dark bands.

## Layout & Spacing
1200px max container, 24px gutters. Sections alternate white and cream with 64–72px vertical padding (40px on phones). Buttons 6px radius, min 44px tall; cards 8–10px radius with a 1px #E3DED2 border and no heavy shadows. Area chips are pills (999px) with a white fill and cream border. Header is a solid navy bar with logo, uppercase nav and a yellow "Call Kris" button.

## Agent Prompt Guide
Build on white with cream alternate bands, navy headings in Jost 500 and Barlow body. Use yellow only for the main call to action (navy text on it), at most twice per screen. Keep it practical: clear service list, real reviews, Kris's number 07740 443180 visible at the top and bottom, and a free-quote form. Use real GB Fixit facts only and label missing photos as placeholders.
